# AgentIA Labs

Clinical AI infrastructure for healthcare organisations, from Barcelona and Valencia (Spain).

### What we build

- **Clinical interoperability:** lab reports and clinical PDFs turned into FHIR R4 IPS, coded with LOINC, SNOMED CT and UCUM, and ready for EHR, LIS and the European Health Data Space.
- **Patient digital twins:** a living model of each person, built from clinical reports and omics data (VCF, FASTQ). It is a decision-support service, not a medical device.
- **Scientific data extraction:** structured data from scientific PDFs and health portals (EMA, ECDC, AEMPS, ClinicalTrials.gov).
- **Agentia Clinic:** clinic management SaaS (scheduling, insurer billing, patient reminders), hosted in the EU or on-premise.

Privacy by design: documents are anonymised before analysis, and on-premise deployment is available. Our demos use synthetic data only.
